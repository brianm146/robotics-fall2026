# line_following

## comparison
Although all my practice and track attempts were able to reach the finish area, the centering for the practice attempt never went off to the yellow area while the track attempts with a gain of 0.08, 0.12, and 0.20 did have its centering go off into the yellow areas. Even though the track attempt with a gain of 0.35 didn't detect into the yellow area, its error was much higher than then practice attempt most of time while the practice attempt started to converge to an error close to 0 during the middle of the straight track. For all my saved practice and track attempts, the speed was 0.16, the roi_top was 0.60, the roi_bottom was 0.95, the hue_low was 140, the hue_high was 169, and the min_fraction was 0.002. However, the practice attempt had a gain of 0.08 while the track attempts had gains of 0.08, 0.12, 0.20, and 0.35. For the practice attempt, the error remained very low near the end on the straight segment. For the track attempts, the error also remained low near the end on the straight segment, but on the first curved segment, the centering was high with oscillation and going far from the center of the track. For the practice attempt, there was relatively stable tracking with some oscillation near the start which was seen by the camera image on the mask moving left and right near the start, but staying more consistent and centered after it passed the center of the track. The error was also lower after the robot passed half of the track and the linear and angular speed commands smoothed out to a consistent value at that point. For the track attempts, there was a lot of oscillation until the straight segment near the end of the track which was seen on the mask with the camera image rapidly going from left to right and sometimes slightly onto the yellow area and only sitting near the center on the straight segment near the end. The error, speed commands, and angular commands were jumping up and down during the oscillation while it smoothed out a bit near the end on the straight segment of the track. When I increased the gain in my track attempts, there was less error and with a gain of 0.35, the camera near veered into the purple area like it did for the smaller gain values.

## limits
One failure from my attempts was the the camera kept oscillating and veered off from the magenta line and onto the yellow area sometimes which began in the control with the robot having a low gain. Some additional sensing and tests that would be needed for a real delivery robot around people is being able to detect people in directions all around it, slow down when it is near people, and being able to take different other routes that don't endanger people if more routes are added for efficiency.
