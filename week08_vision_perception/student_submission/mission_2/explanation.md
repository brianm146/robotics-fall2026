# mission_2

## choice
I recommend the learned detector with learned acceptance threshold of 0.25 for further testing based on my condition table and processing times. Although the processing times for the learned detector is larger than that of the classical detector, the classical detector create false-boxes for both the dim and occluded conditions which can cause the robot to incorrectly detect the mug while the learned detector doesn't create any false-boxes. In the normal_1 foreground example above, the mask IoU is 0.5756 and the dice is 0.7306. A detailed foreground mask does not by itself identify the requested mug  because pixels from other objects near the mug can be included into the mask which will cause it to fail to identify the mug. One important limit of this small image test is that it lacks other kinds of conditions and mugs so for other conditions that are not included and with different types of mugs, the detectors may then fail to recognize certain mugs.

## comparison
For the normal condition, both the classical and learned detector had a correct-box count of 2, a false-box count of 0, and a missed-mug count of 0. For the dim condition, the classical detector had a correct-box count of 1, a false-box count of 1, and a missed-mug count of 1 while the learned detector had a correct-box count of 2, a false-box count of 0, and a missed-mug count of 0. For the occluded condition, the classical detector had a correct-box count of 0, a false-box count of 2, and a missed-mug count of 2 while the learned detector had a correct-box count of 0, a false-box count of 0, and a missed-mug count of 2. The differences that matter for locating the mug are whether the environment is dim or not and whether the mug is occluded or not. For example, in the paired dim_1 images, the classical detector had a large bounding box that included only part of the mug and a large part of the desk because the with the dim environment, many pixels on the desk fit the specific settings for the classical detector while the learned detector had a good bounding box on only the mug and was only missing the handle.
